Paste this into your app's settings at developer.spotify.com/dashboard, under Redirect URIs. It must match exactly.

Since February 2026 Spotify only shares playlist contents with apps for playlists you own or collaborate on. Anything else is locked, however you link to it.

Playback needs Spotify Premium and the Spotify app open on this phone at least once, so it shows up as a speaker.
